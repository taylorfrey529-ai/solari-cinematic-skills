CINEMATIC BIBLE — LAST LIGHT OF SOLARI
Visual system: Solari Cinematic Shot Ladder (one step up or down per cut).
World: A lake-bound citadel of pale stone and gold. Dawn only. No night.
Hero: The Last Sentinel. Elven, long blonde hair, pointed ears, amber eyes,
ornate gold filigree armor, weary resolve — never smirking, never screaming.
Palette: navy shadow, warm gold key, pale marble, lake silver.
Grammar: Start on the establishing shot. Walk the ladder toward the eye.
Do not smash-cut. Intimacy is earned.

BEAT 1 — The world exists without him.
The citadel rises from a still lake. A single figure on the causeway.

BEAT 2 — He is small in the order of things.
Inside the throne hall, columns recede into gold haze.

BEAT 3 — The floor remembers armies.
Circular inlaid gold. Banners. He stands the way a statue stands.

BEAT 4 — The body is the costume.
Head to toe. The armor is a reliquary he still has to wear.

BEAT 5 — Hands at rest are not at peace.
Knees to head, then the cowboy line: mid-thigh, gauntlets, readiness.

BEAT 6 — The face arrives.
Waist-up. Helm pushed back. Grief without tears yet.

BEAT 7 — A decision lives in the eyes.
Chest to head. Then the face fills the frame. Then one amber eye,
with a dying sun reflected in the iris.
